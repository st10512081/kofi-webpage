About Kofi Coffee Shop

More than just coffee, we are about good moments, great flavours and friendly service.

OUR STORY

A Place to Enjoy Every Cup

Welcome to Kofi Coffee Shop, a place built around the simple pleasure of enjoying good coffee, delicious refreshments and meaningful moments.

Who We Are

Kofi Coffee Shop is a coffee shop concept focused on providing customers with enjoyable drinks, freshly prepared refreshments and delicious snacks. We want to create a welcoming environment where people can take a break, relax and enjoy something special.

We believe that a coffee shop should be more than somewhere to buy a drink. It should be a comfortable place where friends can meet, students can study, colleagues can connect and individuals can enjoy a peaceful moment away from their busy schedules.

Our name represents the friendly and relaxed experience we want customers to associate with every visit. Whether you enjoy a strong coffee, a refreshing cold drink or a tasty snack, we want every experience to feel welcoming.

What We Offer

At Kofi Coffee Shop, we aim to offer a variety of products to suit different tastes and preferences. Our menu brings together familiar favourites and refreshing choices.

Our selection includes coffee favourites such as espresso, cappuccino and Americano, as well as refreshing drinks like iced coffee and cold brew. Customers can also explore other drinks such as hot chocolate and matcha latte.

To complement your drink, we also offer snack options such as croissants, muffins and sandwiches. Whether you are looking for a quick breakfast, a light snack or something to enjoy with your coffee, there is something to explore on our menu.
